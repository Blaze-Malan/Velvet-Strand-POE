About Velvet & Strand

Our History

Velvet & Strand was established to provide a dedicated, high-end hair sanctuary exclusively for women. Recognizing a gap in local beauty services, we created a space where women of all hair types can receive specialized, texture-inclusive styling without compromise.

Mission & Vision

  • Mission: To empower women through customized, high-quality hair care in a relaxing, women-only environment.
  • Vision: To be the premier destination for texture-inclusive styling, health-focused treatments, and luxury client care.
