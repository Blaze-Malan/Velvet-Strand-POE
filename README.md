## Project Overview
**Velvet & Strand** is a premium boutique salon dedicated exclusively to providing specialized hair care and styling for women across all hair textures—ranging from Type 1 (straight) to Type 4 (coily and afro-textured). The salon operates with a strict operational rule: *it provides services exclusively for women and does not offer any services for men*.

This website serves as a modern digital platform designed to showcase the salon's texture-inclusive offerings, publish retail product catalogs, provide clear multi-branch contact information, and streamline client inquiries through dedicated digital forms.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives
* **Goal 1:** Provide clear digital menus for both in-salon treatments and retail take-home hair products.
* **Goal 2:** Establish brand authority and trust by displaying expertise across all hair types (Types 1 to 4).
* **Goal 3:** Reduce appointment friction by providing smooth inquiry forms and clear salon policy communications.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Achieve a **15% increase** in web-driven consultation inquiries within 3 months of launch.
* Reduce repetitive phone inquiries regarding service pricing by **30%**.
* Maintain a user bounce rate of **below 40%** via intuitive navigation and responsive design.

---

## Key Features and Functionality
* **Semantic Multi-Page Navigation:** Clean header navigation spanning across six main HTML pages.
* **In-Salon Services Menu (`services.html`):** Categorized listings for treatments like Silk Presses, Texture Cuts, and Custom Balayage with clear durations and pricing.
* **Retail Product Catalog (`products.html`):** Showcase for take-home hair care items like moisture shampoos, deep masks, and scalp elixirs.
* **Interactive Consultation Form (`enquiry.html`):** Form featuring hair profile selection dropdowns to tailor prospective client consultations.
* **Multi-Branch Contact Section (`contact.html`):** Detailed location panels complete with maps and hours for both the City Boutique and Suburbs Sanctuary branches.
